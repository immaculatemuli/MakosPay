Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

In short: FarePay uses your name, phone number and payment records to let you pay matatu fares, to show conductors that you have paid, and to pay vehicle owners. We do not sell your information, and we do not use your location.

Who we are

FarePay lets passengers pay matatu fares by scanning a QR code inside the vehicle, with the FarePay app or the phone camera, using a FarePay wallet or M-Pesa. It is run from Kenya for Makos Sacco. You can contact us on 0742 546 982 or immaculatemuli25@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, phone number and password (stored scrambled, so nobody can read it).
  • Payments: each fare you pay (vehicle, route, drop-off stop, amount, time and ticket code), your wallet top-ups and refunds, and M-Pesa receipt numbers.
  • M-Pesa number: the number you pay from, so M-Pesa can send you the PIN prompt.
  • Camera: used only while you scan a vehicle's QR code. We do not take or store photos.
  • Paying without the app: the M-Pesa number you type on the pay page. Your browser remembers that number and your last stop on your own phone only, so the next payment is quicker; clearing the browser's data removes them. If you later sign up in the app with the same number, those payments appear in your account.

For conductors and SACCO office staff we also keep the vehicle they work on. For vehicle owners, the SACCO office records the owner's name, phone number and, if they choose, bank account details so that they can be paid.

How we use it

  • To let you sign in, pay fares, top up your wallet and see your payment history.
  • To show the conductor of your vehicle that you have paid.
  • To pay vehicle owners the fares collected on their vehicles.
  • To handle refunds, mistakes and questions, and to prevent fraud.
  • To keep accurate financial records, as the law requires.

Who can see it

  • The conductor of the vehicle you paid on sees your name, a partly hidden phone number (for example 0712***678), your drop-off stop and your ticket code.
  • The SACCO office that runs the vehicles sees payments, refunds and passenger accounts so it can manage fares and help passengers.
  • Payment providers (Safaricom M-Pesa, and any licensed payment company we use) receive what they need to process payments, such as your phone number and the amount.
  • We share information with authorities only when the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your account while you use FarePay. Payment records are kept for as long as needed for the SACCO's financial records and for legal requirements, even after an account is deleted, but without your name or phone number.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app: open My account (the person icon) and tap Delete my account. Your name and phone number are removed and you can no longer sign in. Please use or ask for a refund of any money left in your wallet first. You can also ask us to delete your account using the contact details above.

Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, object to how we use it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us using the details above and we will respond as soon as we can.

Keeping it safe

Information is sent over encrypted connections, passwords are stored scrambled, and each person only sees what their role needs. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell you if a problem affects you.

Children

FarePay accounts are for people aged 18 and over. Younger passengers can travel with an adult who pays for them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page, and tell you in the app if the change is important.
