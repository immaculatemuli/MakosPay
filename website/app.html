Get the FarePay app

Pay your Makos Sacco fare by scanning the QR code in the car, book a seat from anywhere, and see your car coming. For Android phones.

Download the app (Android)

Version 6 · works on any network, with mobile data or Wi‑Fi

How to install

  1. Tap Download the app above. If Chrome asks, tap Download anyway.
  2. Open the downloaded file (it is in your notifications, or in Files → Downloads).
  3. Android asks to allow installs from this source: allow it, go back and tap Install. If Play Protect warns you, tap Install anyway. This happens with any app that is not from the Play Store.
  4. Open FarePay and tap Create an account with your phone number.

Already have it? Download again and install over it: your account and money stay.

Pilot test: FarePay is being tested with Makos Sacco. Payments use M-Pesa's test system, so no real money is taken.

iPhone, or no app?

You can book a seat on this website from any phone. Inside the car, scan the QR sticker with your phone camera to pay by M-Pesa without the app.
