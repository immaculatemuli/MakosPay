How it works

Paying a fare takes a few seconds.

Scan the sticker

Every Makos vehicle has a FarePay QR sticker. Scan it with your phone camera, or with the FarePay app.

Choose your stop

Pick where you are getting off. Each stop has its own fare, shown before you pay.

Enter your M-Pesa PIN

M-Pesa asks for your PIN, showing the amount and the vehicle's number plate. Next time, scanning goes straight to the PIN.

Show your ticket

Your ticket code appears on your phone, and the conductor sees your payment straight away.

Book a seat and send parcels

Book your seat before you get to the stage.

Book a seat

Book and pay for your seat on the next Makos car loading at your stage. Show your booking code to the conductor when you get on.

Book a seat

Send and track parcels

Drop your parcel at a Makos office and pay by M-Pesa. Follow it online with its tracking code, and the receiver collects it with a code when it arrives.

Built for everyone on the route

FarePay serves passengers, conductors, the SACCO office and vehicle owners.

Passengers

  • Pay by M-Pesa with or without the app
  • Top up a FarePay wallet and pay each fare with one tap
  • See every payment and ticket in one place

Conductors

  • See every passenger who has paid on your vehicle
  • Check a passenger's ticket code in seconds
  • No cash to carry and no change to give

The office & owners

  • Routes, stops, fares, vehicles and crew in one dashboard
  • Every payment, per vehicle and per day, checked every night
  • Each owner is sent the day's fares by M-Pesa every evening

How the money moves

Every fare is recorded, from the passenger's payment to the owner's payout.

1. Passenger paysBy M-Pesa, or from their FarePay wallet.
2. Fare is recordedAgainst the vehicle, route and stop, with a ticket code.
3. Owner is paidEvery evening, the vehicle's fares are sent to the owner by M-Pesa.
