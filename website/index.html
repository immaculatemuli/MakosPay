Pay your matatu fare with your phone.

FarePay lets passengers pay their fare by scanning a QR code inside the vehicle, with M-Pesa or the FarePay wallet. No cash, no looking for change, and app users pay less than the cash fare.

Scan & pay M-Pesa Cheaper than cash Owners paid daily

How it works

Paying a fare takes a few seconds.

Choose your stop

Pick your route and where you are getting off. Each stop has its own fare.

Scan the QR code

Every vehicle has a FarePay QR sticker. Scan it to see the number plate, route and fare.

Pay

Pay from your FarePay wallet, or enter your M-Pesa PIN when prompted.

Show your ticket

You get a ticket code straight away, and the conductor sees your payment on their phone.

Built for everyone on the route

FarePay serves passengers, conductors, SACCOs and vehicle owners.

Passengers

  • Pay less than the cash fare on every trip
  • Top up the wallet with M-Pesa, or pay with M-Pesa directly
  • See every payment and ticket in one place

Conductors

  • See every passenger who has paid on your vehicle
  • Check a passenger's ticket code in seconds
  • No cash to carry and no change to give

SACCOs & vehicle owners

  • Set routes, stops and fares for every vehicle
  • See all payments, per vehicle and per day
  • Each owner receives the day's fares to their M-Pesa once a day

How the money moves

Every fare is recorded, from the passenger's payment to the owner's payout.

1. Passenger paysBy M-Pesa or from their FarePay wallet.
2. Fare is recordedAgainst the vehicle, route and stop, with a ticket code.
3. Owner is paidOnce a day, all of the vehicle's fares are sent to the owner's M-Pesa.
